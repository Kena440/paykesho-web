How it Works

Simple, Fast, and Convenient

Paykesho makes it easy for customers to access credit and for merchants to boost sales. Here's how it works:

For Customers
  • Sign Up: Create your Paykesho account and get verified.
  • Shop on Credit: Visit partner merchants and choose your products.
  • Repay Over Time: Enjoy flexible repayment plans tailored to your needs.
For Merchants
  • Partner with Paykesho: Join our growing network of trusted businesses.
  • Serve Customers: Allow customers to purchase goods or services on credit.
  • Receive Instant Payouts: Get your funds immediately while Paykesho manages customer repayments.

Whether you're shopping or selling, Paykesho ensures a seamless experience every step of the way.
